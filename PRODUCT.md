## Product

**Name:** Conscious Travel (conscioustravel.id)
**Type:** Boutique eco-tourism platform — Indonesia
**Stage:** Pre-launch (MVP complete, live URL coming)

## What it does

Books and manages sustainable, low-impact travel experiences in Indonesia: curated fixed-departure group tours (eco, wellness, cultural, adventure) and open-dated stays (e.g., The Mangrove House). The site is the storefront, booking engine, and inquiry system combined.

## Audience

- Age: 30–55, professionals with disposable income
- Values: environmental responsibility, authentic local experiences, slow travel
- Geographic: international travelers (EN-speaking), Indonesian domestic secondary
- NOT: backpackers, budget travelers, mass tourism seekers
- Buying mode: considered purchase — they research, compare, read the full itinerary

## Brand Voice

- Calm, assured, knowledgeable — never pushy
- Speaks like a guide who deeply knows the place, not a salesperson
- Uses "journey" and "departure", not "trip" or "package"
- Avoids superlatives: no "world-class", "best-ever", "unbelievable"
- Short sentences. Generous whitespace. Says less than it could.

## Business Model

- B2C direct bookings (primary) — customer pays online via Midtrans (IDR)
- Inquiry-to-quote pipeline for custom/group requests
- Admin panel for tour management, booking tracking, inquiry handling

## Design Lane

**Brand:** Editorial-Atmospheric
**Product:** Premium Minimal

When these two diverge, defer to Brand. This is a brand experience first, a booking tool second.

## Anti-References

Never reference or resemble:
- Airbnb (commodity feel, rounded consumer cards)
- Booking.com (info density, purple palette)
- GetYourGuide (busy, badge-heavy, tour-operator aesthetic)
- TripAdvisor (review-heavy, cluttered)
- G Adventures or Intrepid (adventure brand, rugged/bold)

## Competitor References (study their editorial quality, not their UI)

- Kinfolk.com — typography, whitespace ratio, photography editorial
- Aesop.com — product/brand consistency, typographic restraint
- &Beyond (andbeyond.com) — luxury safari brand, similar premium slow-travel positioning
